Beginner's guide

A simple learning roadmap

You do not need to learn everything at once. Start with the foundations, practise with data, and build one small project.

1

Learn the basics

Understand Python, data, algorithms, and the difference between AI and machine learning.

2

Practise with tools

Use a notebook environment to explore datasets, visualise patterns, and train simple models.

3

Build a project

Choose a small problem, evaluate your result, document your process, and share what you learned.
