Club overview

A student space for ideas with momentum.

Nova Club connects people who are excited by intelligent technology - whether they are coding their first project, exploring prompt engineering or leading a prototype with friends. We learn in public, share useful resources and make practical experiments feel approachable.

Our activities balance technical discovery with human judgement. That means asking better questions, considering who a solution helps, and creating technology that is responsible as well as impressive.

Curiosity Collaboration Responsibility Creativity

What we make room for

  • Explore: test new AI tools, concepts and workflows in a low-pressure environment.
  • Build: move from an interesting problem to a prototype with a team.
  • Connect: learn from peers with different skills, perspectives and goals.
  • Share: present lessons, demos and ideas that can inspire the next builder.

Our direction

Mission and vision

We think good innovation starts with people, not just technology.

Our mission

To make AI learning practical, inclusive and meaningful through peer-led workshops, collaborative projects and opportunities to solve real problems creatively and responsibly.

Our vision

To grow a community of confident, ethical innovators who use emerging technology to improve everyday experiences and contribute positively to a changing world.

How we work

Our club objectives

Every activity should help members move from interest to capability.

Build foundations

Introduce members to AI concepts, useful tools and responsible technology practices.

Encourage curiosity

Create space to question, test and learn from both successful and unsuccessful experiments.

Grow project skills

Help teams turn an idea into a prototype that can be tested, explained and improved.

Connect disciplines

Bring different talents together to imagine AI solutions with real human value.
